Common Topics

Scanning and identifying minifigures

Scan compatible Data Matrix packaging codes beginning with Series 25. Earlier series are catalog-only. Scan support depends on the particular series and packaging code.

Managing your collection

Track the minifigures you own, adjust quantities, and mark favorites. Collection data is stored locally on your device and may be lost if you clear app data or uninstall the app.

Catalog updates

The app retrieves updated minifigure information from its online catalog. For an unrecognized code, check for updates and email support with the series and scanned code.

Ads and privacy choices

Where available, use the app’s Privacy Choices option to review your advertising preferences. See our Privacy Policy for more information.

Optional support purchases

Supporting Scan-Minifigs is voluntary and does not unlock additional app features or remove advertisements. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing.

Still need help?

Email developersims@icloud.com with your device model, app version, and a description of the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive information.
