Scan-Minifigs

Privacy Policy

How Scan-Minifigs collects, uses, and protects information.

Your Privacy

Scan-Minifigs is designed to help you identify and manage your LEGO Minifigure collection.

We aim to collect and process only the information necessary to operate and improve the app.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Information We Collect

📷

Camera Access

Scan-Minifigs requests access to your device camera so the app can scan Data Matrix codes on supported Minifigure packaging.

Camera access is used for scanning and is not used to identify you or collect photographs or videos for storage by Scan-Minifigs.

▣

Collection Information

The app stores information about Minifigures you have added to your collection, including quantities and scan history.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to Scan-Minifigs' servers as part of the normal collection functionality.

⌗

Scanned Codes

When you scan a Data Matrix code, the code is used to identify the corresponding Minifigure in the app's catalog.

The scanned code may be retained locally as part of your scan history.

Catalog Data and Supabase

Scan-Minifigs uses Supabase to host catalog information used by the app.

This catalog may include Minifigure IDs, series IDs, names, images, set information, and Data Matrix code mappings.

The app accesses this information through the Supabase REST API.

The catalog is application data and is not intended to contain your personal collection information.

Information We Do Not Collect

Scan-Minifigs does not require you to create an account to use the app.

We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, or home address through the app.

We do not intentionally collect photographs or videos from your camera.

Scan-Minifigs does not directly collect or store your payment card information. Payment information for support purchases is handled by Google Play and its payment providers.

How Information Is Used

⚙

App Functionality

Information is used to provide scanning, identification, collection tracking, and series browsing functionality.

▤

Catalog Updates

The app may retrieve updated catalog information from our Supabase backend so that supported Minifigures and Data Matrix mappings can be updated without requiring a new app release.

Local Storage

Scan-Minifigs stores certain app information locally on your device, including collection quantities and scan history.

This local information is used to preserve your collection between app sessions.

Deleting the app or clearing its stored data may permanently remove locally stored collection information.

Third-Party Services

Scan-Minifigs may use third-party services to operate parts of the app.

These services may include Supabase for application data hosting, Google Mobile Ads for advertising, and Google Play services for app distribution and processing optional in-app support purchases.

Google Play may process transaction and billing information when you make an optional support purchase. Scan-Minifigs does not receive or store your payment card information.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

📢

Advertising Services

Scan-Minifigs may display advertisements through Google AdMob in supported versions of the app on Android and, when available, iOS.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain information when advertising features are enabled and advertisements are requested or displayed.

This information may include:

  • IP address: May be used to estimate a device's general location and support advertising delivery.
  • Device identifiers: Such as advertising IDs and other device identifiers, subject to device settings and applicable consent requirements.
  • App interactions: Such as app launches, advertisement impressions, taps, and interactions with ads.
  • Diagnostic and performance information: Such as crash information, app launch times, and SDK performance information.
  • Advertising information: Such as advertisements delivered and interactions with those advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may use this information to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve advertising services.

Depending on applicable consent requirements, user choices, and device settings, information may also be used to provide personalized advertising. Where required, consent choices will be requested before applicable advertising technologies are used.

Users may manage advertising preferences through available consent options, their device settings, and Google's advertising controls.

Google Mobile Ads is a third-party service. Information processed through its SDK is subject to Google's privacy policies and applicable terms.

For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings

Optional Support

Scan-Minifigs may offer an optional in-app support purchase through Google Play Billing. These purchases are intended to allow users to voluntarily support the development, maintenance, and continued improvement of the app.

Payment, transaction, account, billing, and related information associated with a support purchase is handled by Google Play and its payment providers. Scan-Minifigs does not directly collect or store payment card information.

Optional support purchases are subject to Google's applicable terms, privacy policies, and payment policies.

Support purchases are voluntary and are not required to use Scan Minifigs or its core features. A support purchase does not unlock additional features, content, or services within the app unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information used by Scan Minifigs.

However, no electronic storage system or method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Scan-Minifigs is not intended to collect personal information from children.

The app does not require an account or submission of personal information to use its core scanning and collection features.

Your Choices

You can choose whether to grant camera permission. Without camera permission, the scanning functionality will not be available.

You can also choose whether to make an optional support purchase through Google Play.

Where advertising consent options are provided, you can review and make choices about the processing of information for advertising. You can also manage available advertising preferences through your device settings and Google's advertising controls.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the app, its services, or applicable requirements change.

The updated policy will be made available within the app.
